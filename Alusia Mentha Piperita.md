 Alusia Mentha Piperita is an evil space tyrant with an immense hatred for her brother, Ponadrum Cinnamomum Verum. She dreams of being the biggest villain of the universe. She is allied with a strange, jelly-like creature from planet [[Kars]], a parasite that devours her blood slowly, but also helps her in many occasions with its strange anatomy and power. She calls it "Kairin".

Together with her crew, known as [[Orion's Wrath]], she has conquered several planets and destroyed millions of civilizations throughout the known universe, is extremely intelligent and ruthless, but a complete idiot when it comes to her family and relationships outside of "work".

When talking about or to her brother, Ponadrum, she reverts to childish insults, screaming and physical violence, a complete 180 from her calm, collected and strategic self. She first appears in a snowy field in [[Kalt Suae]] after her massive ship crash-landed on [[Vostros]] in [[The Divide SIDE A|The Divide]], killing all of her crewmates except Lebadoo, which miraculously survived.

She utilizes the onomatopoeia (???), unique to star system The Who (one of her favorites), to express confusion or disbelief. Its sound is too alien to describe in human terms.
