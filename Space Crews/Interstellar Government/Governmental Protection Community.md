# POST-GANYMETSU ERA
The GPC is the Interstellar Government's Elite Task Force, for secretive missions that can't be assigned to regular officers, but that also are not severe enough to call in a Commander. They are mostly composed of the most skillful the common folk, with humans being a big part of the force due to their ingenuity. They are put into teams of 4 and assigned many types of missions involving secrecy and espionage, such as infiltrating wars to steal intel or blowing up weapons factories across the known universe.
