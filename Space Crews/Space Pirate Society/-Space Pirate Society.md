The Space Pirate Society, also known as the S.P.S; is a recognized organization recruiting any space traveler that has a flag and a bounty. SPS Hideouts can be found in the Space Stations of several solar systems and galaxies, and they are the main group of people hunted by The Interstellar Government due to their amassing of powerful people, including certain members of [[Conquerors|The Conquerors]] or Ex-Conquerors. Their objective is to create a free universe, unbound by the restrictions imposed by [The Interstellar Government](-Interstellar Government).
