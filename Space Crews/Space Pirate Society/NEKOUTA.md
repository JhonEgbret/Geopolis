NEKOUTA is a band/terrorist organization created by [[Conquerors|Conqueror]] Nekocious Neko :3, affiliated to the [[-Space Pirate Society|S.P.S.]]

They have released 5 albums in their lifetime, each garnering massive critical praise, and they are considered one of the greatest and most listened to bands in the entirety of Geopolis. Each album they've done has a different musical genre, but their influences from [[Post-Cun]] and [[Palrata]] are clear in their lyrics and instrumentals. As much as they are recognized for their musical talent, it is undeniable that their true infamy comes from their reputation as the cruelest terrorist organization that has ever crossed Geopolis. The band has a strict moral code, but which has been modified over the years as they matured, and their actions mostly involve mass murder of government soldiers and/or graphic and upsetting brutalization and mutilation of corpses of those affiliated with the Interstellar Government, their main enemy.

They are separated into 3 different groups with a very clear hierarchy. Firstly comes The NEKOUTA Crew, consisting of the main five. Then, The NEKOUTA Fighters' Guild, a group of 400 skilled fighters from several planets and solar systems, acting as the band's soldiers and bodyguards. Lastly, The NEKOUTA Lesser Guild (as of the end of the Megalo Era renamed the "Policing Guild"), acting as the cannon fodder until their superiors organize around the threat, as much as the rest want to deny it. 

If the two guilds protecting NEKOUTA fail at their respective missions, the threat is handled by the band itself, a most undesirable outcome for whoever it is that decided to cross their path. The NEKOUTA Crew have never lost a battle ever since that night in Litrkan, the night that ended it all. At this point, the only people that could outmatch them is if all the other Conquerors banded together against them, and even then they would find a way to overcome them, with few losses.

They travel around the universe singing their experimental songs, alternating between different musical styles every years
