Truth Society Azure, commonly known as the "T.S.A.", is a [[Strixitania|Trixan]] student group formed in [[Trouxas|Trouxas]], [[Stonewall]] at [[Edwington Gar High School]] by Kumie Tachibaru. A crew of conspiracy theorists and/or nutcases, which dedicates itself to create an army against corrupt politicians and monarchs in The Divide.
