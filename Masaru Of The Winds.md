Masaru Of The Winds is a massively powerful entity, and one of the only magicians to be part of The Conquerors. He is distant from the [[Magician Community]], and banned from interacting with it in any shape or form. Masaru is known for his manipulation of the winds and incredible force, withstanding attacks from other conquerors without a scratch. He is highly ambitious, and one of the Conquerors to claim the most territory ever since the [[Ganymetsu Era]].

It is unknown from what species he belongs to, although his appearance is of a short, skinny human with no hair whatsoever on his body or head, a hunched back, pale, almost bluish skin, giving him the appearance of a corpse, sunken eyes and a serious expression on his face at all times. He is highly pragmatic and his true motives will always be a true mystery.
