The creature from Yuanf, commonly known as "Saltman", is a tall and terrifying humanoid entity with the head of a traditional glass salt shaker and body akin to the size and build of a strong 20-ish years old male, always wearing casual human clothes. Its true origin and gender is unknown, but it refers to itself as a male human, and cannot interpret its own reflection. Saltman feels a strong sense of compassion for others and wishes to help everyone he meets, despite suffering severely for it.
