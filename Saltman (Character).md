The creature from [[Yuanf]], commonly known as "Saltman", is a tall and terrifying humanoid entity with the head of a traditional glass salt shaker and body akin to the size and build of a strong 20-ish years old male, always wearing casual human clothes. Its true origin and gender is unknown, but it refers to itself as a male human, and cannot interpret its own reflection. 

He has spent his formative years living on a farm, closed-off from society at large, being taken care of by ex-members of the Zokan terrorist group, that have hid away from the rest of society, and are the only people that had not shunned him up to this point. They taught him everything he knows

Saltman feels a strong sense of compassion for others and wishes to help everyone he meets, despite suffering severely for it. Many of his adult years were spent acting as a vigilante for the city of Anevia
