Hello, if you are reading this, you've stumbled across my worldbuilding endeavors.

I have been making this universe since the start of 2024 if I recall, being originally made for a story called Saltman, but I eventually started to expand beyond the place where the story ended up, and it ended up like this.

It is severely disorganized, as it was meant to be seen in the program I use, called Obsidian, and I use a plugin to back up my annotations on it to this repository. Eventually, once I get at least one quarter of it finished, I'll find a way to sort things.

Articles may wildly change in very little time, as nothing is set in stone yet. Very recently I changed the 7 Universal Strikes to only 3, for example.

I will update this probably for as long as I live, adding more to it as my real life knowledge in politics and geography grows. I won't use it for anything in particular just yet, but I plan making some books and even VNs in this universe.

Enjoy!
