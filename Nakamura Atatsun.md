Nakamura Atatsun is The Interstellar Government's [[Commanders|7th Commander]] (out of 10) and a master of the "[[Yaminisatsu]]" Martial Arts Style, one of the deadliest in the universe. He was born and abandoned in the slums of the planet of [[Alixatra]], being forced to confront the dark reality of his life at a young age, and was taken in as an orphan by the [[Magician Community]].
