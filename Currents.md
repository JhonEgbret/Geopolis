The Universe of [[Geopolis]] can be described like a box inside a box inside a box or a room full of mirrors facing eachother, wherein each "box" or "mirror" is an alternate plane of existence, what is called a Current, an energy that flows like water. The Currents are (generally) impassable barriers that separate several planes from eachother, such as The [[Underworld]] or The [[Pale]], which cannot be accessed from Geopolis or [[Errata]].
