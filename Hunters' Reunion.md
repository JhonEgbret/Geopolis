The Hunters' Reunion is an event that occurs once a year in the [[-Interstellar Government|Interstellar Government's Frigate Number 1]], wherein all the most important figures of the universe (Monarchs, Presidents, Governmental Forces, The 1%, Space Pirates in and out of The [[-Space Pirate Society|S.P.S;]] etc.) reunite in order to discuss manners of dealing with, as well as categorizing, the ascension and fall of the people considered [[Conquerors|Conquerors or Ex-Conquerors]]. The Interstellar Government sends ships to all known planets in order to investigate and collect their most powerful figures for this one special day, offering free space travel.

It is known to be a chaotic and brutal event, with lots of discourse from different groups as much as they are segregated in order to not cause conflict within the Frigate. Many times powerful people have tried to crash the reunion in order to give their opinion on the matter, but always meet their end at the hands of [[Commanders|The Commanders]], who protect the entire location, inside and out, from enemy attacks. Only once the enemy's opinion got the right to be heard, and it changed everything.
