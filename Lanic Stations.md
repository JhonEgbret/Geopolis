The Lanic Stations, first created in the planet of Oitis, are technologically advanced, small-sized, modular constructions similar to gas stations in our world. They can be shrunk down to the size of a briefcase and carried around, as well as being able to resist extreme environmental conditions such as weather, gravity and any form of damage inflicted. Their purpose is to connect planets to the [[INS]] (Interstellar Net System) and effectively universalize the world, for good or bad.
