Jerojero Lambarin is the Second Son of [[Queen Vanabara]] of the [[Lambarin Royal Family]], and was born in the planet of [[Tchaldaq-Nabir]] in [[Palagna Castle]]. He is a friend of [[Nekocious Neko|Nekocious Neko :3]], and provides miscellaneous instruments for [[NEKOUTA]] due to his contact with many instruments throughout [[Geopolis]].
