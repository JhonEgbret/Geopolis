Halrebar is the home planet of [[Conquerors|Conqueror]] [[Nekocious Neko|Nekocious Neko :3]]. It is one of the most populated and crowded planets in the known universe, as it possesses, ever since the [[Ganymetsu Era]], one of the biggest and most important commercial districts in the universe, the district of [[Aludfried]].

The planet is separated into two sides, living districts and commercial districts, with examples of living districts being cities such as [[Bashic]], [[Feldanar]] and the human district of [[Nintachi]], and commercial districts, those being more plentiful, being Aludfried, [[Gafried]], [[Helika]], [[Shash]], [[Ternata]] and [[Ninhodo]]. Its architecture is brutalist and industrial, its cities being only composed of massive, sometimes titanic apartment complexes, wherein there are small, cramped rooms made for the sole purpose of sleep, as any food and necessities such as medication and clothing are chosen for you by the corporation you work for. There are no options for enrichment, no art, musical instruments, radio, television, holovision, [[Arkadot]], videogames, telephones, cartograms, not even furniture to be placed, and all rooms are in constant 28/70 surveillance and equipped with thousands of hidden security cameras. All previous methods of housing have been demolished in place of the apartment complexes, which at times can reach heights of 880 metres, taller than the Burj Khalifa in our world.

Its climate is temperate, with occasional rain at certain points of the year, and very frequent thunderstorms, that are utilized as an energy source in various commercial centers.
