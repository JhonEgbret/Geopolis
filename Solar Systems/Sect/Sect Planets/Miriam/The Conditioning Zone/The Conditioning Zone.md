The Conditioning Zone is the term used to denominate a specific area ranging from 3,433 to 3,985 kilometers in the planet of [[Miriam]], wherein it is maintained at a constant -600 degrees Alethis by the planet's core. It is the Zone responsible for most of its geopolitical events and the area that harbours the most people in the planet at all times. It was officially rated one of the least interesting natural phenomena in the solar system by the [[Larn Interstellar News Press]].

There are 4 Factions currently battling for territorial conquest in the Conditioning Zone, each with a main goal in mind for its citizens and utilizing the most underhanded and inhumane tactics in order to claim territory. Most of its citizens live in its major cities in fear of the next battle, and many have fled to the Warm Sea in search of paradise, despite its danger.
