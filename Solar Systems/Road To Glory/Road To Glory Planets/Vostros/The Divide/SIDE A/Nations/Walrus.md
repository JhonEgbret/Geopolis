Walrus, also known as Bonerland, is a nation located in The Walrus Archipelago on [[The Divide SIDE A|The Divide]] and populated by a people called The Walruses. They are a semi-anarchist nation with no plans for expansion or domination. Its citizens are peaceful, but Walrus is known as the strangest nation, even compared to [[The Fool]], as they are completely esoteric and have no laws in what is considered their territory. Their objective is unknown, and they have a very strong alliance with [The Sunfarers](Sunfarers.md).

Their leader is a skeleton named Boner, who has a vast level of power and connections, his most important one being his lifelong friendship with [Tomoe Gervogyan](Tomoe Gervogyan Williamson Barbosa Baghdasar). He is known to be lighthearted and endorse complete unregulated freedom, consequences and all. His second in command is Nanaon, a highly intelligent girl, who was chosen by him due to her skill in leadership and command. Although public opinion of her is not very favorable, she is able to walk safely in the streets among the people.

The islands that belong in this complex territory are known to be highly colorful and full of equally peaceful creatures, although their neighbor island, Kalt Suae, is full of some of the most predatory creatures known to man.
