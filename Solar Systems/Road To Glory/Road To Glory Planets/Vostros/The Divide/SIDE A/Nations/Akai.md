Akai is the most violent nation of [[The Divide SIDE A|The Divide]], and the most focused on amassing military power. It used to be The World Capital of Musse before its eventual takeover by King Khan.

It is one of the smallest nations, occupying only the coast and being surrounded by [[Sunfarers]] territory which they plan to overtake.

Their ruler, King Khan, is a completely ruthless and horrible man who resides in its capital, Gabagoolland, and rules it with an iron fist. He has exiled his own son, Phoenix, from his kingdom.

Akai is a very technologically advanced nation in terms of weapons and weapon design due to many technological inventions being passed on by [[Strixitania|Strixan]] travelers and weapon enthusiasts.

It has been able to maintain its small territory for a long time due to being surrounded by the pacifist Sunfarers, but they sometimes have to defend themselves from [[The Fool]] constantly raiding their territory.
