The island of Carcino is the uppermost island in [[The Divide SIDE A|SIDE A of The Divide]], and the only island located in The [[Sea Of Death]]. It is a highly tribal and animalistic island, and the second smallest nation. Its leader and motivations are unknown. Its native animal, the [[Kapu]], caused the epidemy of [[Kaputerica Pestis]] that almost destroyed most of civilization in The Divide.

Their people only live to survive and work almost like a hivemind, hunting for food, killing the diseased among them and celebrating their hunt after the day is over. Their mysterious leader is an outsider that has took his time to establish himself in the community as if he was always there, and virtually nothing is known about him despite the fact he was able to cross the Sea Of Death unscathed.

There is virtually no record of the island having ever existed, and it is treated as but a myth passed down for generations. It is not counted in any maps due to none having been able to confirm its existence.

Carcino's Key Color is Black.
