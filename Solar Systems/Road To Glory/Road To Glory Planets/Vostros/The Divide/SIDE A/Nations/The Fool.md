The Fool is a nation located in [[Heart Island]], in [[The Divide SIDE A|SIDE A of The Divide]]. Its citizens are similar to circus creatures in appearance and weigh as much as a piece of paper, therefore being able to float and jump at incredible speeds, as well as walk on water.

They can leave their island with their lightweight clown car crossing the water to more territories, but are closer to [[Akai]] than the others, which makes them an easy target.

They are extremely brutal, murdering and pillaging every village they come across outside their territory.
