The Sunfarers are a pacifist nation in SIDE A of [[The Divide SIDE A|The Divide]] with the aspiration to restore nature and the Sun's beauty to the entire world. Their nation was founded in 1989, 3 years after the Technological Revolution of Dogland that rapidly began to pollute the environment. They have no sworn enemy, but vow to shut down the factories that have been created throughout the world in order to keep The Divide's natural beauty utilizing negotiation and equivalent exchange.

### Leader

Their leader is [[Tomoe Gervogyan]], also known as the most powerful human being in the known universe. She has trained to her fullest since childhood and has superhuman abilities as well as a terrifying resolve. If she wanted to, she could destroy [[Vostros]] itself with her massive power. Her main objective is to create a sustainable and united world, where none are separated by race, gender or sexuality and the planet has no risk of being destroyed with our own actions. Of course, that is a highly idealistic mindset, and for it to be maintained many sacrifices and deaths had to occur, to none of her citizens' knowledge.

She is, deeply, a good person, who fought her entire life to be able to rule her kingdom and trained restlessly since a child in order to achieve her dream, going through several adventures and meeting several people in the process, some including [[Marcille Strixitania]], when she was a mere princess, and the ruler of [[Walrus]], Lord Boner, which she has a peaceful and agreeable relationship with in modern times. Despite her intentions, the world, however, might not be so kind to her.

However, Tomoe would risk it all for her kingdom, be it friendships, loves, dreams and even her own life. She has lived for many years and aged an extremely minute amount, and she will live for many more protecting the citizens that she considers her family up to her final moments.

### Religion

They worship the God of the Sun, Arkos, and have built several temples throughout their territory for prayer and "spiritual healing". They have a highly sought-out for medicinal plant called Sunov, which they keep secure in the coastal temple of Solaris, being observed and tended to by their 4 Sun Monks.
### Citizens

They are happy and cheerful people compared to the other nations, and have an astounding amount of territory in SIDE A of The Divide and even some in SIDE B due to Tomoe's ability to cross The Seam.

Their society is highly inclusive of all types of people and their citizens do not judge anyone based on race, much to the excellent education provided by the Sunfarers' schools, known to be the most impartial and unbiased way of consuming the history of The Divide. They are also extremely welcome with newcomers, and always give gifts and such to anyone who visits their land.
### Land and Architecture

Most of their land is composed of plain terrain, with some islands or landmasses possessing hills or forests, such as Nemmish Woods or The Singing Hills. They are home to many whimsical and adorable creatures, which all live peacefully being protected by them. There are almost no carnivores in Sunfarers territory, and their ecosystem and naturalism are commendable.

Their architecture is as focused as possible on letting natural light illuminate their environment. As there are no tectonic plates in most Sunfarers territory and a very minor chance of natural disasters, their windows and "streetlights" are illuminated by a type of glass that reflects a massive, but safe, amount of light, leaving most of its citizens with a tanned complexion due to the exposure to the sun, even causing them to be treated differently in other nations such as [[Strixitania]] and [[Akai]]. This has never been a problem until recent times, however.
### Capital

Tomie Country, sakura trees, sun shining, the throne of Tomoe at the very top.
