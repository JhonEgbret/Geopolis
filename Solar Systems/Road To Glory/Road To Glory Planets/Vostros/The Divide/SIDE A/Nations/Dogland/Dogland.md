Dogland, not to be confused with Lower Dogland, is the weakest nation of [[The Divide SIDE A|The Divide]] in terms of military power, and it is medium-sized. They have a long, complex story and are the most technologically advanced nation of all, being responsible for the Dogland Industrial Revolution that took The Divide by storm.

Their main habitants are animals of the [[Canis]] species. , which have higher intelligence than human beings and have advanced technologically and politically in a pace much faster than them.

The people of Dogland love food more than anything, and the key to befriending a Canis is going on a nice plentiful lunch with them. They love running and are mostly fit and fast, even the older ones.

Their key color is Light Blue, and their key font is Fallouty.
