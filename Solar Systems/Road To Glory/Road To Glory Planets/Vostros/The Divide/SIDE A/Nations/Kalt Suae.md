Kalt Suae is a winter nation located at the very bottom of [[The Divide SIDE A]]. It is strictly focused on partying and celebrating the end of the world. Its citizens are strange, and their island has the most perilous fauna in the entire planet of [[Vostros]].

Their customs involve celebrating the slow arrival of the God of Death, Cyros, to their homeland. Due to the [[Technological Revolution of Dogland]], the rapid industrialization of the civilized world caused severe amounts of pollution into the environment which coalesced at the Kalt Suae nation, resulting on the effects of rapid global warming and slowly chipping away at their land. They seem to embrace this slow death as their whole culture has always been based around it, even before they got to witness firsthand the impact the rest of The Divide has left in their territory and planet.
