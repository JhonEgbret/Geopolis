The Azure, also known as Azure Corps or just Azure, are a subdivision of The Interstellar Government responsible for the protection of the most powerful nations in The Divide's both sides.

The only forces they cannot interfere on are the actions of [[Elites|The Elites]], [[Commanders]], [[Conquerors]], and certain crime families and organizations.

Its rules remain the same as The Interstellar Government's, with slight variations to fit the longstanding Divide Laws. Azure, like The Interstellar Government, utilize bounty posters. The prices on criminal's heads vary as a manner of indicating the threat level of the outlaw shown, either posing a threat to Azure as an organization, the citizens of The Divide or even the universe.

Azure Corps' headquarters is located on a private island East of the territory that once belonged to [[Aofuria]], [[Olvephus]] and other nations. It is known as The Elite Island, facing the Sea of Death and The Aruturi Archipelago, and is responsible for housing the group known as The Elites and providing protection, entertainment and resources to them.
