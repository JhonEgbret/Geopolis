[[The Divide SIDE A|The Divide]] is home to several Royal Families, each ruling their own kingdom in or outside of it. Some Royal Families have been exiled from their territory by other forces (See: [[Amarill Ciccer|Queen Amarill]]) or have lost access to their original kingdom (See: King Khan).

## SIDE A

The most infamous Royal Family of Side A of The Divide is the Strixitania Royal Family, and second being the Gabigool Family. There are also known ex-royalty crime families such as the Sudokubo family of assassins and the Sunfarers family only known as "The Falecceri".

### THE STRIXITANIA ROYAL FAMILY: $4,340,000,000 BOUNTY - FROZEN DUE TO COLLABORATION WITH THE AZURE.

The Strixitania Royal Family is commanded by the Queen [[Marcille Strixitania]], being 64 years old and and past her prime in terms of power level. She is an egotistical and authoritative old woman, who despises and executes anyone that dares oppose her ideals or insult her nation.

The Queen possesses 2 daughters, named [[Eleanor Strixitania|Eleanor]] and [[Alice Strixitania]], respectively. Eleanor is the next to the throne and will be happily married to the Prince of [[Aofuria]], Ogeki, when her mother finally passes. Alice is known as the "pawn". She is controlled and manipulated by her mother and sister and forced to live a life of poverty in order for them to "teach her a lesson". She despises her family and kingdom, but cannot escape without being persecuted and executed by the hateful citizens. Alice is granted a small form of protection with her bodyguard, a strong and intimidating man named [[Garouche]].

### THE GABIGOOL ROYAL FAMILY: $6,450,987,245,844 BOUNTY - CONSIDERED AN "IMPOSSIBLE TARGET" TO AZURE.

The Gabigool Family is not from planet [[Vostros]]. They have come from [[Lonalictus]], arriving on Vostros since ancient times, and have lived planning their takeover ever since being shamed by the human race.

Years later, King [[Khan Gabigool]], at his prime 405 years of age (Lonalites live up to 500 years uninterrupted), carried on with his ancestors' plans and in the morning of [redacted] took over [[World Capital of Musse|The World Capital of Musse]] and murdered half of the kings in the SIDE A of The Divide, including King [[Bartholomew Strixitania]] and many others. He then took over the capital and utilized it to instill his supremacy, turning it into the nation of [[Akai]]. He has a son named [[Phoenix Gabigool]], but he was since then exiled from his father's kingdom, making his way to [[Strixitania]] and living in secret from humanity and its Royal Family, which would kill him on sight if ever spotted. His son currently lives in the HQ of the [[Truth Society Azure|T.S.A.]]
