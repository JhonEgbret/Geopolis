The Elites are an organization created by Durinki W. after his intimidating standoff at The Conflict of Law, it consists of protecting only the "truly special and worthy" members of [[The Divide SIDE A|The Divide]], and also the wealthiest and most dangerous ones. It is above [[Azure Corps]], and below [The Commanders](Commanders).
