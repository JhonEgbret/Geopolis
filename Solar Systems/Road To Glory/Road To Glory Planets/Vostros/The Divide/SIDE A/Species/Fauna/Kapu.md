The Kapu is a bird native to [[The Divide SIDE A]] and the main culprit behind the [[Kapu Plague]] of the [[Fallscure Era]], responsible for wiping out half of The Divide's population. It is a relatively large brown bird resembling a Kiwi Bird from our world but with the average height of a penguin and larger, more mobile feet. It is generally docile towards other species, but if a bird is infected with the plague its brain will rapidly deteriorate, making its movements uncontrolled and its behavior violent towards any nearby species.
