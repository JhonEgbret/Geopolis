Khan Gabigool is a tyrannical false king from The Divide's SIDE A and the leader of the Akai Nation. He is known for his violent and erratic behavior, having shaped the future of The Divide through his bloody and evil actions. He believes war is the only true constant of life and that it should be kept that way for eternity, his ideals and personality reflected by the warlike fervor of Akai and its citizens.
