Lupino P. Alerma is the most famous elite and the Don of the Lupino Crime Family. He is a dangerous and highly intelligent criminal who leaves no trace of his crimes and has no limit to his evil. His evil knows no bounds, although he has a genuine side to him which rarely reveals itself. Lupino is a charismatic man from the outside, but all that join his family eventually get to know the true evil and malice hiding within his heart, with over 600 confirmed kills and counting, as well as several horrible things he has done, with no motive behind his crimes besides his lust for power.

At this point in his career he is untouchable, and has assembled, throughout his 40 years of life, a Crime Family that leaves all others in the ground, and one of the most powerful groups of people living on [[The Divide SIDE A|The Divide]]. He is above Drinken in terms of power, be it political or physical, being part of a subspecies titled [[Lupis]], with characteristics closer to wolves than domestic canines.

One of his best friends is the innocent and clueless Weepasta Ordos, a fellow elite which he adores speaking to, most probably for malicious reasons considering their age difference.
