Weepasta Ordos is a known elite and evil billionaire with an expensive collection of magical artefacts that she doesn't use in any way imaginable as she is a stupid teenager. She travels the skies with her private aeromobile, bought with the soul of 40 children executed systematically during [[War of The Josuns|The War of The Josuns]], and has fun in several different locations with no consequence, as she is part of [[The Divide SIDE A|The Divide's]] elite and richest, which ultimately control all.

She is the newest and youngest person to ever be considered an elite in the history of The Divide, as she obtained her fortune very suddenly and quickly.

## Backstory

Weepasta's planet of origin is not known, but she was forced to work at the circus since she was born due to her strange, alien appearance compared to regular humans.

She received her education at the circus and was taught all manners of mathematical equations related to perfecting her puns and slapstick humor, all being taught to her by the circus performers themselves, who raised her like their child. It was a very strict process that reinforced her connection to her circus, which she worked in for most of her life. It is unknown how she got rich, but most of her excessive spending and vanity comes from her past insecurities subconsciously resurfacing, as well as her need for attention. This is what started her relationship with a fellow elite named Lupino P. Alerma, who was the only one of The Elites to treat her like they were on the same level, and which she quickly grew attached to.
