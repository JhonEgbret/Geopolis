Tomoe Gervogyan Williamson Barbosa Baghdasar is a solemn and quiet woman now responsible for the nation of [[Sunfarers|The Sunfarers]]. Considered too much of a threat to be part of [[Conquerors|The Conquerors]], as well as being number one on [[-Interstellar Government|The Interstellar Government's Watchlist]], she is a force to be reckoned with in other parts of the universe, but her past is mostly unknown to the people of The Divide and its surrounding solar system. **She is the most powerful human being in the universe.**

Tomoe loves her kingdom most of all things, and loves nature secondly. She despises anyone who causes harm to nature and has made a personal promise to a long-lost friend to never use her powers for evil. Tomoe is an incredibly smart person, but she will never let others know, instead opting for a clueless, innocent persona. She is incredibly strong-willed due to her horrible childhood and past, and will never crack under pressure. Tomoe wants to protect life at all costs, and does so by being a pacifist and attempting to be on good terms with all nations.

## Abilities/Techniques

### Learned Skills

She is extremely proficient with firearms, sniping, medical support, aeromobile piloting, ship navigation and many other skills, as well as having full knowledge of most languages in The Divide and out, being extremely persuasive and intimidative, having extensive knowledge related to the world and universe she lives in and most of all, a power that few in the universe possess due to its horrible implications.

### **Black Magic.**

[[Black Magic]] is one of the rarest forms of magic in the universe. It is strictly forbidden and can only be utilized by being in (or being in possession of someone going through) immense physical or mental pain, as all Black Magic spells are derived from that energy.

There are few people in the world that know of it, let alone teach it, as all of the books that taught them or any variation of them have been destroyed completely a long, long time ago. All of its spells are either grotesquely inhuman, highly destructive or a mixture of both.

**There has never been a person or spell powerful enough to counter Black Magic, and there never will be one.**
