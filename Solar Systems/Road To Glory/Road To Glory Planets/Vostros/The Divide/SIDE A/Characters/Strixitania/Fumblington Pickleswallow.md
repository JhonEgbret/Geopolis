Fumblington Cobblebottom Pickleswallow is a charming, fat old rich man, and one of the main business owners on the nation of [[Strixitania|Strixitania]] of [[The Divide SIDE A]], being considered part of The [[Royal Guard of Strixitania]] due to his control over weapons and ammunition factories in all of The Divide's territory.

As of the start of the [[Megalo Era]], Fumblington is trying to find a cure for the sudden outburst of [[Kaputerica Pestis]] (Or the Kapu Plague) in The [[Butchery]], leaving workers sick and mass deaths, of which his only concern is the halting of weapon production at the midst of the most important event being planned for in Strixitania's history.
