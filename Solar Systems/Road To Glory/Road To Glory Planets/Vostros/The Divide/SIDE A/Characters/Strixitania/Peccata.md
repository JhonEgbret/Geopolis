Peccata, is a strange young girl from [[Trouxas|Trouxas]], [[Stonewall]] that frequents  [[Edwington Gar High School]] (occasionally) and watches over the T.S.A. for unknown reasons. She is a pale and ghostly-looking woman with a strange fascination for religious sin, death and desire.

Peccata is constantly crawling rooftops to observe the "commoners", and has gotten in trouble several times from doing so, generally being swatted with a broom like a rat until she falls off. As she constantly reads some of the only remaining [[Black Magic]] books in existence she is a force to be reckoned with, although she is too dumb to understand most spells.

Her main particularity comes from a power she discovered during her childhood that she has never used ever since. She does rituals to appease the God of Death every single day at midnight, in order to keep her life for another day and to not ever be discovered by another magician.
