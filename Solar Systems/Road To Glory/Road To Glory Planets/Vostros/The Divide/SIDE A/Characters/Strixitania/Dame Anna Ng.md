The crew she began assembling at 14 is now known worldwide as a group of some of the most dangerous individuals in the Seenlands, but she has split from them since hitting 20. born in 1978

== Backstory ==

=== Childhood and NEET phase ===
Tomoko Kuroki was born in Aydingway, Strixitania and had a relatively normal life, until a tragic event in her school which resulted in her becoming socially inept. After her 14th birthday, her brother traveled to Sunfarers in order to get a better education (Because he is actually intelligent unlike her. She is special), leaving her alone once again. 

After weeks of going back to being a stinky NEET and being surrounded by hatred, she decided to transform her overwhelming sadness and loneliness into a hatred for the world. On the 6th of November, 1991, she stood in front of Azure HQ and declared war against every nation that wasn't Strixitania, making her life's mission acquiring the perfect crew in order to make Strixitania dominate the universe.

=== Forming "The Trix" ===
The first member of her crew was famous musician Ringo Starr, known for his work as the drummer of famous UK band "The Bentoids". She met him during a party inside the Big Benny to commemorate the return of famous UK guy "Jorty Forty" from the Brazilian Wars, which scarred him for life and gave him severe PTSD when they lit the candle to his "Welcome Back" cake, and he ended up shooting 5 children, confusing them with the child soldiers commonly used during the Brazilian Wars' 2nd period. She and Ringo fought with Jorty, her using her terrible smell to knock him out and Ringo finishing him off using his iconic drumsticks stand. After the fight was over, Ringo and Big Benny decided to join her crew, inspired by her courageous act of heroism, and also because she needed to take a fucking shower.

After assembling the first 2 members of her crew, she decided to travel to the most drab city in the UK, Bristol, in order to plan out their strategy for forming the perfect crew, as it was way too boring for anyone important to notice their scheming. Big Benny had the idea to forge a Pearl Harbor-style attack on the Buckingham Palace, but Tomoko turned it down, as she loved the japanese too much to make them suffer once more (she hates America) and the fact that even the sole mention of the Queen made her wet. Ringo's plan was also not very useful, which involved the murder of his former bandmate John Lennon and The Catcher in The Rye (The plan will not be disclosed for legal reasons), and it was also turned down.

After walking around in Bristol for a while and analyzing its population, she decided to put up an ad for a group orgy on 4chan and waited in a Fish and Chips shop until someone showed up. She would end up meeting a strange femcel girl named Maya Ongezellig, who wanted to be part of the event to help with her extreme social awkwardness and NEET behavior. Tomoko understood that sentiment and decided to teach her social skills, but failed miserably and ended up radicalizing her even more and making her part of the crew

All of them decided they needed to move on from Bristol onto greener pastures (literally), but before finally settling on a Headquarters somewhere else, they needed to make one more move. To attract more attention to Tomoko's cause, they took a group picture in Bristol together and made it into a poster advertising their new HQ in the city of Westminster (before even getting there), attached to it a phone number written in a secret cipher that only few people know about (mostly 4chan nerds), spreading it everywhere they could in Bristol and adjacent cities as the crew traveled to the place where their iconic base of operations would be created.

Amidst their traveling, Maya Ongezellig, due to her epic hackermode computer skills and her iconic robot sidekick XJ-9 (Jenny Wakeman), noticed something odd. A strange cartoon crow had been following them in secret since the beginning of their journey, emmiting radio signals in morse code to an unknown location about their every move and conversation. Big Benny safely disposed of it utilizing his iconic time-freezing powers and his giant clock hands, but it had already been too late. Someone or something was listening in on all their plans and strategies, possibly even setting up an ambush on their future Headquarters location.

Despite their hesitation, they continued on their journey, watching out for anything that might be spying on them. In this paranoid state they killed over 234 pigeons and crippled over 542 children who crossed their path, "Its all in the name of big britty ye" Big Benny said when asked about the matter, not willing to disclose any further information.

When they finally got to Westminster, what they noticed is that the entire city was practically empty besides a single place where there were few people, the pub. Among the ones drinking there were Barry (63), a Sick Bear, 2 mysterious men and Tomoko's own brother, Tomoki, all having fun as friends. Tomoko was greatly confused at her brother's presence, and when asked about it he only said "kill yourself" and left, the others only staring in silence with a drink in their hands.

Now, all these strange brits needed to do was wait as their cause grew more popular and the recruitments were finally over, then begin planning their next move, which never ended up happening.
