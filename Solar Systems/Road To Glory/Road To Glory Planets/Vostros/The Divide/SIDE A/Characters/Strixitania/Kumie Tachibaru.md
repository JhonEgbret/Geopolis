Kumie Tachibaru is a student at [[Edwington Gar High School]] in [[Trouxas|Trouxas]], [[Stonewall]], and the responsible for the formation of the T.S.A. Kumie is a laid-back and charming young girl, although also being a bit mentally ill, who will dedicate her life to live freely amongst the other nations and combat the corruption of The Divide, as she made a promise years ago to an old friend. 

She possesses a [[Spesfleur]] on top of her head, but has no knowledge of its meaning.

Kumie was a normal girl from [[Akai]], born in the town of [[Annodoro]], in the country of [[Oerodito]], [[Bou]]. She lived with her mother and father which she loved very much, and went to school every day. Eventually, her city was caught up in the [[Akai W.A.C.K.A. Revolution Protests]], at the middle point of the [[Armistice Era]], and soon the [[war that would overtake Bou as well as part of the nation]]. Kumie, at 5 years old, moved with her family to a forest area in the northernmost part of [[The Revolution Field]], where they made a small makeshift tent for her and her mother to live in, as the town was no longer safe. She was kept in protection and homeschooled by her mother, a renowned Akai astrologist, which taught her all she knew about constellations, planets, stars and the like, as they would sit and gaze at the starry sky every night, their only comfort from the horrors that had taken the nation. Kumie's dream was to see the stars, to see space itself and all its planets, but she knew it would never come true.

A certain day, when she was 11 years old, her tent was surrounded by strange men, and her mother spoke to them in a desperate voice, in a language she could not understand. When Kumie's mother came back to the tent, she told her to always follow her heart, to never give into temptation and to run, run as far as she could from her home, leaving her behind. Kumie tearfully followed her mother's advice, she ran, ran, ran, and when she looked back she saw the tent she knew as her home being burned to ashes along with her mother, agonizing in pain, by enemy Akai soldiers. They spared her. It was at that moment she realized the true nature of humanity, and the first time she was truly alone.

Slowly, she learned to live by herself in the forest, straying from the path of predators and picking scraps of food and items from dead soldiers which would wash up in the Revolution Field. At 13, she gathered enough food and resources to make her trip out of the Revolution Field, which took her approximately 1 and a half years, but it was to no avail. The forests kept getting longer, and hope was running shorter. Eventually, her food ran out, and soon her body would run out of stamina and go limp from exhaustion. As she looked at the constellations in the night, in the middle of the forest, starving and alone, she let herself cry for the first time. She cried, cried and cried like she had never done before. For her town, her friends, her father, her mother, her dreams, her life. She thought the forest would be her final resting place, under the starry sky.

Eventually, the unexpected would happen, and a massive spaceship would abduct her and take her away to space. It was the ship of [[Ignatius Polanka]], which was visiting Akai in search of young girls to fulfill his yearly tax to the [[Mahou Shoujiety]], in accordance to the rules of the [[-Space Pirate Society|S.P.S.]] She was nursed back to health, and her unconscious body was shipped to the Mahou Shoujiety HQ. She woke up in an empty, dark room, with a plate full of delicious food, which she promptly devoured, and out of the shadows stepped their dark leader Hokurado. He would then invite her to the Mahou Shoujiety, explaining for hours on end what her purpose was and how she could fulfill her dream of traveling the universe alongside his crew. It was an unrefusable offer, and she rapidly accepted, getting used to her new life and situation over the course of a few months, although suffering with grave PTSD and mental issues from her time in the war. The first few missions were tame, wherein she made friends and used her survival skills to fight lesser monsters, such as blobs, goblins, small cuboids and animals in order to save people. She would never expect, though, what her next mission would entail.
