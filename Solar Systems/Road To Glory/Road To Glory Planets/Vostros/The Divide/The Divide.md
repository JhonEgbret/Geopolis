The Divide is the name used to describe, in the planet of [[Vostros]], 2 landmasses located in the Subdermal Sea and separated by [[The Seam]], with the left being named SIDE A and the right SIDE B. At the top of the seam and the wall covering The Divide there are [[The Outerlands]].
