Vostros is one of the most important superplanets to Universal History, housing several species such as the [[Canis]] and [[Yborgo]]. Its surface has a flat coverage of stone, called [[The Outerlands]], but its most characteristic aspect are the two large craters that house the Subdermal Sea and a mass of islands, mountains and tectonic plates, called [[The Divide]].

Few can survive the harsh weather and incredibly dangerous fauna and flora of The Outerlands, as they are considered inhabitable and for that reason have rendered the area useless to the eyes of The [[-Interstellar Government|Interstellar Government]], their perception and observation of the planet are solely dedicated towards the important universal figures standing inside The Divide, which for some inexplicable reason seems to attract power and influence

Vostros, ever since the [[Bodement War]], has been considered a planet of influence by The Interstellar Government, allowing it to participate in the [[Hunters' Reunion]].
