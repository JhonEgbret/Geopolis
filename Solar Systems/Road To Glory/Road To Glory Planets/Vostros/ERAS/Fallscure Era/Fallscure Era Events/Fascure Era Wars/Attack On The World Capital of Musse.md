The Attack on The [[World Capital of Musse]], located in [[The Divide SIDE A|SIDE A of The Divide]], was an act of terrorism executed systematically by The [[Gabigool Royal Family]] during the [[Fallscure Era]] with the objective to kill all of the kings and queens in SIDE A of The Divide. It begun at 3 AM in the morning during preparations for a [[Hunters' Reunion]] and ended at 7 AM in the same morning the moment [[Khan Gabigool]] declared himself King of the New Nation to the public, which he would name [[Akai]]. [[Amarill Ciccer|Queen Amarill]] was the only survivor, and her report of what took place there is the only one that can be trusted, as Akai over the years has spread propaganda throughout The Divide attempting to erase the event from history.

The events occurred during and after the attack would forever impact SIDE A of The Divide and its repercussions can be felt to this day.
