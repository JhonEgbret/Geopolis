The Attack on The [[World Capital of Musse]], located in [[The Divide SIDE A|SIDE A of The Divide]], was an act of terrorism executed systematically by The [[Gabigool Royal Family]] during the [[Fallscure Era]] with the objective to kill all of the kings in SIDE A of The Divide. It begun at 3 AM in the morning during preparations for a [[Hunters' Reunion]] and ended at 7 AM in the same morning the moment [[Khan Gabigool]] declared himself King of the New Nation to the public, which he would name [[Akai]].

The events occured during and after the attack would forever impact SIDE A of The Divide and its repercussions can be felt to this day.
