The Fallscure era began with the death of the [[Sabre]] of the [[World Capital of Musse]], succumbed to the plague, which marked the beginning of the mass hysteria surrounding the [[Kaputerica Pestis|Kapu Plague]], an illness that in this era would wipe out half of the [[The Divide]]. The citizens of the World Capital along with the nation of [[Strixitania]] would then undergo a long grieving process aptly named Fallscure, or the all of The Divide into obscurity, darkness.

Trading and commerce were at an all time low, causing massive conflicts and wars for safe land and food around the nation of [[Olvephus]] and [[Konatsuka]], with times being more bleak than ever before. The people of Strixitania developed an obsession with death, and many would sail to unknown seas, with many going on to form the Walrus nation and certain ones in the [[Aruturi Archipelago]], or being unlucky enough to end up in [[Carcino]]. [[Azure Corps|Azure]] would utilize the times of great weakness to gain dominance over [[The Divide SIDE A|The Divide's SIDE A]] territories, especially Olvephus and Strixitania, with the promise to wipe out the [[Kapu]] species, main transmitter of the disease, thus establishing itself as one of the big players in The Divide very rapidly. Going down South to [[Kalt Suae]] or up North to Konatsuka, both nations' closed down policies would end up containing the spread of disease, a fact that would unfortunately not last long.

The rapid extinction of the [[Sunov]] plant would also owe itself to the Fallscure Era, as in times of great desperation more and more people would seek out the plant, thus causing [[The Great Devastation of The Sun]], which was responsible for the death of most of the Sun Monks and the near exiction of the species. In the same vein of environmental disaster, the nation of [[Dogland]], [[Upper Dogland]] as it is now known as, would undergo through its worst period with the [[First Great Rot]], in which a [[The Rot|massive cloud of disease and death]] would occupy the territory, causing massive famine and disease which practically destroyed the nation, with knowledge of it being limited to tales centuries old, warning of its return. 

While the people of The Divide were suffering, a war on a much larger scale was happening right above them, with the Duel of The Winds being the forefront of universal discourse at the time, wherein the [[Conquerors|Conqueror]] [[Personagem]] fought his fellow conqueror [[Masaru of the Winds]] for 6 days, around the start of the Fallscure Era, in a territorial conflict. Masaru had recently usurped the Throne of the [[Vetticus Family]] in the planet of [[Socis]], located in the neighboring solar system [[Sect]], and had plans to invade The [[Road to Glory]] with his army, until he was stopped by Personagem, who claimed the solar system was his territory, protected under his flag. Personagem landed the first blow to Masaru's army in the planet of [[Ebrippe]], and the battle had begun, with their uninterrupted fighting lasting 4 days, until they declared a temporary truce in order to read the newspaper, which lasted approximately 2 hours. During the truce, Personagem already figured out the way to defeat Masaru, and after the fighting resumed, brought him to the planet of Vostros, which led to the creation of the rock formation now named "[[The Conquerors' Realm]]", achieved with the explosion of an entire mountainous range into a platform for battle, which raised sea levels and massively increased wind speeds for over a decade after the fact. When the sun finally rose, at their 7th day of fighting, the victor was decided. Based on reports from [[W.A.C.K.A.|W.A.C.K.A;]] Personagem, holding up Masaru's bloodied and impaled body, uttered one last word, forever marking the history of The Road To Glory. 
Grinning, he said: 

" *Pathetic.* "

The rumbling of the seas and strong winds massively benefited Azure Corps on its conquest. It also benefited The Rot, which grew stronger than ever before, forcing the [[Canis]] to relocate to the South, thus forming [[Lower Dogland]], and the ones that had stayed to adopt a primitive way of life.
