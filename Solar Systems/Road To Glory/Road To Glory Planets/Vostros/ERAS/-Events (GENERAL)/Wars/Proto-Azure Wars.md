The Proto-Azure Wars are a series of different wars occurring in [[The Divide]] over the course of the [[Fallscure Era|Fallscure]] up until the end of The [[Amacy Era]], resulting in the infamous Bodement War and the infiltration of [[Azure Corps]] into [[The Divide SIDE B|SIDE B of The Divide]], as well as its inclusion in the [[Hunters' Reunion]].

The following wars have all been part of the Proto-Azure Wars:

- Initiation War
- Pestis Bioterrorism Conflicts
- Jaffités Wars
- Institution War (Known as Kingsfall War to non-Azure)
- [[War of The Josuns]]
- Sunfarers-Strixitania Power Crisis
- [[Bodement War]] (Known as Unification Day to Azure)
