The Proto-Azure Wars are a series of different wars occuring in The Divide over the course of the [[Fallscure Era|Fallscure]] up until the end of The [[Amacy Era]], resulting in the infamous Bodement War and the establishment of Azure Corps into SIDE B of The Divide, as well as its inclusion in the [[Hunters' Reunion]].

The following wars have all been part of the Proto-Azure Wars.

- Initiation War
- Pestis Bioterrorism Conflicts
- Jaffités Wars
- Institution War (Also known as Kingsfall to non-Azure)
- [[War of The Josuns]]
- Sunfarers-Strixitania Bellic Power Crisis
- [[Bodement War]]
