The Bodement War is one of the most brutal and massive conflicts that has ever occurred in [[The Divide]], its impact and consequences on [[Vostros]] being felt to this day.

The Conflict began at [place and time whatev], with the sudden arrival of a [[Yaghvardi]] species member into the city. After giving its prediction time of 49 years, it left just as quickly as it had arrived. [[Mr. Azure]], in a reckless move driven by his grief over the eventual loss of his empire, and with plans to expand while they still have time, launched an all-out attack on the capitals of [[Dogland]] and [[Strixitania]] as well as the cities their respective leaders were visiting, at the time [[Keinudy Jagd]] and [[Marcille Strixitania]] respectively.

After the unexpected attack, which resulted in the assassination of Keinudy and Marcille's paraplegia, the forces of Strixitania decided to fight back with all of the resources they had accumulated with the [[Sunfarers]] surrender of all their Firepower during the [[Sunfarers-Strixitania Bellic Power Crisis]]. The war's first victim was a lost [[Akai]] immigrant named [[Abuki]], shot down by Azure after being confused for an enemy due to the Strixitanian colors on his clothing. After the end of the war, his death would become a symbol for the rebellion that would strike the nation of Akai decades later.

**No living being inside the city of [[Ardming]], [[Chiuanna]] that day would ever be able to predict the inhumane battlefield that the land they built, lived in and cared for so long ago was about to become.**

At the end of The Bodement War, [[Azure Corps]] were the victors. It is still unknown whether they won with outside help or through the sheer power of [[Elites|The Elites]], forced to fight by their side. Outside witnesses of this terrible war, [[Orion's Wrath|The Orions]], who were visiting Vostros at the time trying to chase down the Yaghvardi that had delivered the news, said they had never seen such needless bloodshed in their lives. Their Captain, [[Alusia Mentha Piperita|Alusia]], was especially fascinated by seeing her old friend, [[Limme Kimbler|Limme]], after so long, and committing such horrible crimes.

Alusia soon spoke to Kimbler a week after the war had taken place, at the very end of the [[Amacy Era]]. Inside The Elites' Mansion, with a view overlooking the millions of victims begging for answers at their gates. The two had a lengthy conversation, in which she asked him to join her crew. He refused, as he was afraid. They then spent most of their time discussing [[Kanpaian philosoph]]y, questioning what could drive their species to this breaking point, and Alusia left without a trace. That was the last time they would ever see eachother.

The operation was overlooked by [[Colonel Adam Sanderson]] of the very first division of Azure Corps. 2 years after the war, he was arrested for negligence, genocide, unlawful use of firepower, destruction of property as well as trespassing nation borders. He committed suicide in the jail of [[Lagumasya County]].

After the victory of Azure over The Divide was reported by The Orions, The Interstellar Government considered Vostros a planet worthy enough to partake in the [[Hunters' Reunion]], one of the most important universe-wide events of all.

The [[Amacy Era]] soon ended after this war, approximately 1 week, 3 days and 13 hours after victory was declared. This event is celebrated by Azure-protected countries as "Unification Day".
