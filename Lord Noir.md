The most powerful demon in the universe. [[Personagem]] is his last creation sent to Geopolis, and his best. Not much is known about him, but the words "Barathrum" or "Noir" have become a massive taboo universe-wide because of his ancient and evil actions, without most people even knowing why or who he was.

Very little is known about Noir himself, with his presence being limited to a few historical records, and evidence of his existence being destroyed due to fear of his return. It is told his rise to power began around the end of the Worthwhile Era, at a time of great distrust and fear after the First Universal Strike. Its planet and species of origin are unknown, and his technological prowess is centuries ahead of its date of origin, with even the simplest of its machinery being impossible to replicate even with today's technology. 

2 theories prevail on the topic of his origin, but with fickle evidence regarding both. The first related to the planet of (), claiming that Noir is part of their pantheon, known as The God of Death. His symbol and the inexistence of a God representing Death in their pantheon may point to its veracity, but his amount of technological prowess and knowledge of other languages may prove otherwise, as well as the main evidence disproving it, that it was able to exit the planet. The second is one of the most incredible, but the most widely known. It claims it to be a time traveler, who was able to grow his notoriety quickly by traveling to one of the most vulnerable Universal Era, and that now plans to destroy it, seeking twisted revenge. It is disproven by the recently unearthed records of his humble beginnings on the [[Wacatac]] Solar System, now mostly oral, as well as the impossibility of time travel. 

Reports of his appearance are equally vague, but with certain point of consensus. It has been reported by all sources to have massive black horns extending out of his head, and dead eyes with no pupils and that strike an unbelievable fear into the soul of his enemies and his victims. Reports of his height go from 8'2'' tall to up to 70'8'', but the consensus is that it is known to be tall enough that it will never be able to look up to see anyone. His symbol, known as the [[Mark of The Dead Sun]], is usually used to refer to it in place of his name, and is present universally on nearly every preserved book and document in Universal History mentioning it, as any history books or novels with his name have been destroyed, and all copies incinerated. It utilizes a glyph from the planet of (), representing the God of Gods, as well as the Dead Sun, representing the darkness it has brought to the universe. His voice and appearance have countless conflicting reports. Some say its voice is gravely and frail, masculine, while others say it is loud and booming like that of a god, or distorted in an unrecognizable and unintelligible manner, and there are countless reports of its language, although it is all but confirmed that it knows several languages from around the universe, including dead ones, from universes it's extinguished.

Noir's ship, called the [[Barathrum]], is the largest ship in the universe, and houses millions of the most powerful soldiers and monsters in Geopolis. It is the approximate size of an entire Solar System, fitting for a creature of its scale, and houses the Mark of The Dead Sun in its front doors. It accepts visitors, armed or otherwise, as it knows anyone intending to do them harm will never be able to do so. [[Ganymetsu]] is a girl that has been proven to visit them whenever big developments are happening, and she is a great friend of it.
