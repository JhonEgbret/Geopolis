Saltman is a comic series created by Alishi Jhonapus. It is a tale set in the [[Megalo Era]] of [[Geopolis]] in the planet of [[Vostros]], most specifically [[The Divide]], and revolves around the characters of [[Saltman (Character)|Saltman]] and [[Linlin]], in their journey to discover her true parents and in the process getting involved (by accident) in several universe-wide events such as the founding of the new [[Orion's Wrath]] and the consolidation and rise to power of the [[T.S.A.]] as the "New [[NEKOUTA]]".
