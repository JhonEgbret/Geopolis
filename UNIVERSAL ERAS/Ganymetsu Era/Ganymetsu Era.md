The Ganymetsu Era, one of the most troubled Eras in Universal History, owes itself to its main culprit, a young girl by the name of Ganymetsu -Gan, which held all the secrets of the known and unknown universe. At the year of 1433, in Glory Calendar, the first sightings of an unlicensed space traveler began around the region of the Dural Solar System, with rumors of criminality being suspected, but the second the IG was sent to intercept the rogue traveler the signal disappeared, leaving only a trail of smoke behind. A few weeks later, the inciting incident for the entire Era would occur, with the sudden explosion of a Petraco Mine around the region of the Moon of Tephades, and the same signal of the rogue space traveler being detected, as well as the same smoke trail. The IG did little in its way to pursue the culprit, but private investigators from the planet of Lydra were sent by the miners' families to uncover the assassin, and they followed the smoke trail, a journey that sounded like days turning into weeks, then months, then half a year, and only 1 of them returned. What the man, a tall and lithe rookie named Waral, said after his return, was not encouraging either. He reported seeing a small, big-eyed human woman, who had red irises, skin as pale as snow, hair as black as the void, and was about the size of a 10-year old girl. She rode on a small ship with the design of a brown boot, with the glyph () carved on its side and its sole, in an unknown language. The families were confused and some were enraged, but one of them was alerted by the description, the Filacna Archea family, who recognized the description from old myth, who spoke of a young traveler with boundless knowledge who signaled the end of the eras, and the power to destroy the universe. Needless to say, they were highly alarmed, but were only made fun of by the IG when trying to explain the threat this posed. The eldest son of the Filacna family, Guildur, decided to take matters into his own hands and, armed with the family heirloom, the powerful Yllv-2 military-grade energy weapon, banned from use due to its inhumanity and destructive power, he set out to take revenge, and possibly even save the universe itself...
