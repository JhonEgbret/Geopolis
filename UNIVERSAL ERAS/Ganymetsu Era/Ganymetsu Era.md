The Ganymetsu Era, one of the most troubled Eras in Universal History, owes itself to its main culprit, a young girl by the name of Ganymetsu -Gan, which held all the secrets of the [[Known Universe|Known]] and [[Forgotten Systems|Unknown Universe]]. At the year of 1433, in [[Glory Calendar]], the first sightings of an unlicensed space traveler began around the region of the [[Duarl Solar System]], with rumors of criminality being suspected, but the second the [[-Interstellar Government|Interstellar Government]] was sent to intercept the rogue traveler the signal disappeared, leaving only a trail of smoke behind. A few weeks later, the inciting incident for the entire Era would occur, with the sudden explosion of a [[Petraco]] Mine around the region of the Moon of [[Tephades]], and the same signal of the rogue space traveler being detected, as well as the same smoke trail. The IG did little in its way to pursue the culprit, but [[private investigators]] from the planet of [[Lydra]] were sent by the miners' families to uncover the assassin, and they followed the smoke trail, a journey that sounded like days turning into weeks, then months, then half a year, and only 1 of them returned. What the man, a tall and lithe rookie named [[Waral]], said after his return, was not encouraging either. He reported seeing a small, big-eyed human woman, who had red irises, skin as pale as snow, hair as black as the void, and was about the size of a 10-year old girl. She rode on a [[small ship with the design of a brown boot]], with the glyph () carved on its side and its sole, in an unknown language. The families were confused and some were enraged, but one of them was alerted by the description, the [[Filacna Archea]] family, who recognized the description from old [[myth]], who spoke of a young traveler with boundless knowledge who signaled the end of the eras, and the power to destroy the universe. Needless to say, they were highly alarmed, but were only made fun of by the IG when trying to explain the threat this posed. The eldest son of the Filacna family, [[Guildur]], decided to take matters into his own hands and, armed with the family heirloom, the powerful [[Yllv-2]] military-grade energy weapon, banned from use due to its inhumanity and destructive power, he set out to take revenge, and possibly even save the universe itself...

After another half of the year, Guildur was finally able to find the ship that started it all, and speak to Ganymetsu. Her blood-red eyes stared at his soul as he drew closer and closer, pointing his Yllv-2 at her, but in a twist of fate, Guildur felt compelled to ask her the question he had been wondering his entire life: Why he was even born. All the girl could do was answered, and answer she did, then explaining the entire history of the universe, from forbidden secrets to the truth behind history books, from the dawn of creation to the dawn of man to the fall of man, all leading up to Guildur's birth, and all only to answer "I don't know". Upon hearing her explanation, a beautiful, lengthy and methodical tale of beauty, tragedy, friendship, betrayal, love and hate, Guildur began to tear up. -Gan told him he had nothing to be ashamed of for crying, and stood by his side as he tried to unpack the scale of what he had just heard, and wondering how something so beautiful could come out of someone he had despised for so long. After a while, Guildur came to his senses, and asked her if and why she had done what she did, the explosion that started it all, receiving the answer "I was merely returning things to how they should be. You would not understand, given you would never be assigned to this role." After receiving such a vague answer, Guildur was infuriated, but could not bring himself to pull the trigger after all he had heard. As he wondered what to do, she extended her hand to him and asked if he wanted to have fun. The man was offended and shocked at the same time, but grabbed her hand in response in an act of sudden compassion, letting her drag him into the space-boot and fly him away to a far-off moon, leaving his ship behind floating in space. 

The two landed at the dwarf moon of [[Reticuli]] to a beautiful flower garden, the likes of which he had never seen before, especially not on a moon so small you could walk through the entire thing in around 3 minutes. At a table there was a small object she called a "phonograph" playing a song he had never heard before, which she called the "Air on G String" by someone with a [[Johann Sebastian Bach|name he could barely pronounce]]. Around them there was nothing but the looming presence of the planet of [[Kiarwa]] on the horizon. Ganymetsu then danced elegantly as the darkness set in for the moon, moving like he had never seen a human move before. It was like Reticuli was her stage, and he was the audience, until she reached out her hand and asked him for a dance, and he realized this moment was truly special. The 2 of them effortlessly moved in sync as the sweet melody played, and Guildur thought about life, existence, humanity's burden and if the [[original humans]] were still out there, somewhere in the deep confines of space and existence. For many moments he had forgotten what he came to do there in the first place, entranced by her charm and mystery. The two danced for so long it felt like days had passed, until exhausted, they fell on the bed of flowers and he saw her smile for the first time. They gazed at the stars for a period of time that felt like weeks on end, without saying a word to each other, only exchanging vague glances, until the girl got up, being reminded of her responsibility. She turned off the music and told him to go back to his planet, as she had things to do and couldn't bring him along, walking towards her boot and offering to give him a ride back to his ship, now floating aimlessly in space. Guildur said goodbye to her, and they left the same way they had met in the first place, without saying a word. They would never meet again, or so he thought.

After saying her goodbyes, Ganymetsu made her way to a solar system at the very end of The [[Forgotten Systems]] to meet up with a being of extreme importance. She knocks on the door of the [[Barathrum]], where [[Lord Noir]] is waiting for her. Noir has always been protective of Ganymetsu, treating the young woman almost as his daughter, her being the only thing he has ever shown affection to. As news of her spreads throughout Geopolis due to Guildur's carelessness, Ganymetsu realizes her life will never be the same. Noir swears to protect her within the Barathrum and to destroy any who come in her path, but Ganymetsu needs to go, even knowing she might be targeted by people all over the Universe with not very good intentions.
