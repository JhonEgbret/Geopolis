The Ganymetsu Era, one of the most troubled Eras in Universal History, owes itself to its main culprit, a young girl by the name of Ganymetsu -Gan, which held all the secrets of the known and unknown universe. At the year of 1433, in Glory Calendar, the first sightings of an unlicensed space traveler began around the region of the Dural Solar System, with rumors of criminality being suspected, but the second the IG was sent to intercept the rogue traveler the signal disappeared, leaving only a trail of smoke behind. A few weeks later, the inciting incident for the entire Era would occur, with the sudden explosion of a Petraco Mine around the region of the Moon of Tephades, and the same signal of the rogue space traveler being detected, as well as the same smoke trail.
