The Duel of The Winds was a conflict during the [[Lax Era]] between [[Conquerors]] [[Personagem]] and [[Masaru of The Winds]] over the territory of The [[Road to Glory]] Solar System, Personagem's territory, which Masaru planned to invade following his claim of the [[Sect]] Solar System. The war lasted 7 days, with a 2 hour truce in between, fought by Masaru's Royal Army against Personagem, alone, which then went on to win despite the numbers, the battle ending on the planet of [[Vostros]], on [[The Divide SIDE A|SIDE A of The Divide]].
