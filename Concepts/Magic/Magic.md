Magic is a power born from the souls of every living thing in the universe. It is a form of energy that moves the soul and allows it to function, and it can grow bigger depending on how one trains it. Most people spend their whole lives without training their innate magical power, and therefore it is a power only known of by few.

## **MAGICAL ABILITIES

Magic can concede several kinds of unique abilities beyond one's physical strength, they can be learned by utilizing spell books or created by their own user. Magic is usually taught in [[Magic Schools]] across the universe, with most being kept secret. All mention of Magic and/or the [[Magician Community]] is to be omitted from any historical records (besides their own) and all forms of media such as newspaper, movies, television, holovision and such. Failure to comply with their requirements may result in harsh, harsh consequences. 

[[Magic Books]] are only printed in certain [[far-off moons out of the public eye]] and usually only 1 or 2 copies are shipped to planets containing Magic Schools utilizing their own way of transportation. They contain a list of beginner spells, that are now common to most solar systems due to the books' proliferation, but it is known that what makes every magic user unique is the spells they've created outside of the books. 

The amount of spells and the type of spell a Magician can cast are practically infinite, or at least as much as their memory can allow them. From spells that can harm one's body to modify it there are infinite possibilities, and they are mostly classified in the amount of Soul Power they utilize, which is classified by analyzing a spell's destruction level combined with the severity of its drawbacks when cast by an unexperienced users. Drawbacks can range from light headaches to the sudden explosion of one's brain or heart, and in very severe cases (with spells that have *mostly* been banned) the destruction of one's soul. Destruction levels range from the power to lightly scratch someone to destroying entire villages (and of course, with spells utilizing Black Magic, the power to destroy entire Solar Systems), be it with an explosion, a toxic gas, a barrage of bullets or a variety of other sadistic spells.

## **REINCARNATION

All beings in the universe reincarnate, taking on new forms through the millennia, and Magic Users who have trained their Magic past a certain point are able to transfer their energy to their next life, and can even learn their past lives' memories if they really really want to. Death was trivial to Magicians for many years, until the birth of [[Black Magic]], at an unspecified time in [[Universal History]]. It is something feared by Magicians ever since its dawn, and for good reason.
