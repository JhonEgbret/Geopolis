A "Forgotten System" is a Solar System that, among many, is so distant from the [[Solis]] that it falls behind the [[Border]] and is considered no longer under the jurisdiction of the [[-Interstellar Government|Interstellar Government]]. A Forgotten System is so far away from all trade routes and civilization that it has either been a footnote on [[Universal History]] or not mentioned at all, with some exceptions. The people from the Forgotten Systems are few and far between, and the region is home to several small [[Space Pirates|Space Pirate]] groups due to low surveillance.
