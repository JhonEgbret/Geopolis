The Commanders are the [[-Interstellar Government|Interstellar Government's]] best of the best, designed to be a Force to beat all other Forces, and to go toe to toe against [[Conquerors|The Conquerors]]. The Commanders are the most powerful lawful beings in the [[Known Universe]], and their stories and scars have no limit to them, although, ever since the [[Universal Strike|3rd Universal Strike]] their alignment in the scale has changed.
