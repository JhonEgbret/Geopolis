Kinrin is a resistance group responsible for several events in Universal History and that have liberated several civilizations and even planets from corrupt leaders, dictators or even forces of the Interstellar Government. They are extremely powerful and have powerful allies as well, despite not being affiliated with the Space Pirate Society.
