**Hell-Radios** are small, portable radios that were made by the [[Yurtnir Family]] in order to communicate with devils in [[The Underworld]]. The only one seen in the known universe was bought by [[Vostros]] [[Elites|Elite]] [[Weetorio Lenox]] for [[Lenox Artifact Heritage|his artifact collection]], although he forgot who he bought it from.

The Radio has 2 frequency knobs, which indicate Latitude and Longitude, respectively. The radio functions as a kind of Satellite which roams around the territory of The Underworld and transmits sound and translated speech to its owners. It can be tuned to the frequency of hotspots in the territory of The Underworld, although, as it has only been seen by few, there is very little reliability to which frequencies are ideal or not.

The older models of Hell-Radios possess a massive design flaw that was fixed in later models. If a Devil's power is too strong, said models have the capacity to overheat and explode in a terrible [[Black Magic]] Cloud of death, and can cause **extreme** levels of damage to its surroundings and user if a rare situation of the sort occurs, with destruction of the [[Soul]] and [[Universal Breaches]]. Said design flaw thus makes them highly sought for as weapons, especially its first model, which has been lost somewhere on The [[Forgotten System]].
