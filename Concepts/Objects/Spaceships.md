Spaceships are space-travelling vehicles used since the Beginning Era that can travel great distances, being of all shapes and sizes, although they are inaccessible for most due to requiring a series of important licenses to use, put into place by the Interstellar Government to avoid interfering with the ecosystem of planets which haven't reached the same level of technological advancement as others. Ships are put into classes from F to SS (with the SS class being reserved only for Commander ships)
