The LOEs are bipedal machines annotated in the name of [[Laupatter]], built for endurance and self-defense in hostile environments such as uninhabitable planets or regions full of [[Space Pirate]] activity. The first generation of machines is known for several design flaws, having an 87% rate of failure and a high risk of explosion, its development only continuing due to Laupatter’s high reputation and his connections with private investors such as [[Narmiller Knox|Narmiller]], [[Weetorio Lenox|Weetorio]] and most of all, three members of the [[Commanders]]. The second generation is highly improved from its antecessor boasting enhanced protection modules and a higher range of attack, as well as its main draw, the new version of the [[Tipan]] Cannon, one of the most expensive and powerful weapons ever made in the [[Known Universe]]. 

The Ultimate LOE, known as LOE-ST MK3, is a gravity-bending titanic war machine capable of leveling entire planets, built after the [[Universal Strike|Third Universal Strike]], however it has been deemed too powerful to be used by any being, and all engineers and scientists behind its production have mysteriously disappeared or gone into isolation, some afraid of what they had created, others proud. It is equipped with Ultra-Tipan MK2 Cannons on each side and moves bending gravity itself to its will, being able to cross distances insanely fast regardless of its immense weight.
