Centuries ago, at the depths of one of humanity's most grueling facilities, an Artificial Intelligence was created by the [[Original Humans]] in order to waylay information through the usage of leylines across the Universe. The Interstellar Net System is a foreign [[OS]] illegally utilized by several Solar Systems to communicate, the byproduct of those humans' efforts alongside the planet of [[Oitis]], now considered the original creators of the program. It can be obtained through illegal [[Cartonication]] (CC's/codes) to certain [[Lanic Stations]] spread out across [[Geopolis]].
