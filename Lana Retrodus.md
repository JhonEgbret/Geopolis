Lana Retrodus is a young and troubled magician, grizzled and traumatized from her years of adventures and her seemingly slow aging. She lives alone in a manor, which she built herself, overlooking an abyss. She has no knowledge of [[Black Magic]], but is considered one of the most infamous people ever related to the Magician Community.

She has made many connections over her years, but has forsaken all of them in the name of living in solitude for the rest of her life. After being hit with extraterrestrial knowledge by the wandering creatures known as [[Yaghvardi]], Lana has never remained the same. The will to remove those horrible images of doom from her mind caused her to join the [[Magician Community]], but she was never able to remove them entirely.
