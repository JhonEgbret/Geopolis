Liverdaria Catto, commonly known as her alias "Nekocious Neko :3", is the founder and captain of terrorist group/band NEKOUTA. She is a sociopathic anarchist woman with the dream of destroying all that is sacred and holy in Geopolis, and bringing down all forms of government. She has mellowed out since her teenage years, wherein she wreaked havoc, being known as one of the main factors towards the 3rd Universal Strike and respective conflicts afterwards.
