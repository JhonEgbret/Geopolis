The World Government is a federation of over 170 affiliated nations that governs most of the world. It was founded by an alliance of 20 kings 800 years ago and has since vastly grown in influence. The offspring of those 20 kings are considered royalty and titled "Celestial Dragons", except for the first monarch of the Nefertari family, who unlike the other 19, chose not to become a World Noble and move to The Holy Land of Mary Geoise for mysterious reasons.

It is publicly administered by a governmental council known as the Five Elders, and its main combat forces are its 3 Admirals, 2 Fleet Admirals and the Shichibukai, which are a group of 7 pirate crews that do jobs for the government in exchange for a frozen bounty and significantly more leeway to commit crimes (See: Doflamingo's takeover of Japan/Brasília).

The only forces they cannot interfere on and neither can the Interstellar Government are the actions of The Four Emperors, powerful pirate crews (See: Orion's Wrath, The Strawhats), crime families (See: Tomingelli International Crime Family, The Epsteins) or organizations (See: Unity Corp, SCP, The Empire, Upper Iowa University).

It is the Earth subdivision of The Interstellar Government, but its rules remain the same. They, like The Interstellar Government, utilize bounties as a manner of indicating threats to their agenda, the world or potentially the universe.

The biggest threats to The World Government follow the watchlist written by The Interstellar Government, focusing on threats confirmed to be currently habiting Earth.
